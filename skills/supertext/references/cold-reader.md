# Cold reader

Used in step 3.1. A cold reader checks whether the text works for someone who has only the text — which is exactly the situation of its real reader. Who reads, and what to do without a subagent, is in "Checks by someone who wasn't here" in `SKILL.md`.

## What the reader receives
- The finished text, as it will be read.
- One line on who the real reader is (for example, "an engineering manager who wasn't in the discussion"), only if the text doesn't make sense without it.
- Never the conversation, the Text plan, the central idea or the objective.
